
# Tapestry Holistic Healing

## Healing Beyond the Physical

At Tapestry Holistic Healing, I believe that true healing extends beyond the physical body.

Just as a tapestry is woven from many interconnected threads, our wellbeing is influenced not only by our physical health, but also by our emotional, mental, energetic, and spiritual health. When one area becomes out of balance, it can affect the whole.

While modern healthcare often focuses primarily on physical symptoms, many people intuitively recognize that stress, grief, trauma, life transitions, relationships, purpose, and spiritual connection can profoundly influence how we feel.

Energy Medicine offers a gentle yet powerful way to support healing on these deeper levels.

Energy Medicine works with the body's subtle energetic systems to encourage balance, flow, and healing.

Many clients describe their sessions as deeply relaxing, restorative, and transformative. Some experience a profound sense of peace, clarity, emotional release, or renewed connection to themselves. Others simply leave feeling lighter, calmer, and more grounded.

Each session is unique and unfolds according to the needs of the individual.

## Energy Medicine & Acupuncture

Many patients choose to incorporate energy medicine into their acupuncture treatments.

For those seeking a more immersive experience, Energy Medicine sessions are also available as standalone treatments.

Like every thread within a tapestry, each aspect of who we are matters. When those threads are brought back into coherence, we often discover greater health, resilience, meaning, and wellbeing than we imagined possible.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## What conditions do you commonly work with?

Acupuncture may help support a wide variety of health concerns, including: 

- Chronic and acute pain
    
- Neck, back, shoulder, hip, and knee pain
    
- Headaches and migraines
    
- Fertility support
    
- Hormone imbalances
    
- PMS and painful periods
    
- Perimenopause and menopause
    
- Digestive concerns
    
- Stress and anxiety
    
- Insomnia and sleep issues
    
- Fatigue and burnout
    
- Metabolic health concerns
    

If you are unsure whether your condition is a good fit, please reach out.

## Does acupuncture hurt?

Most people find acupuncture much more comfortable than they expect. The needles are extremely thin and many patients barely feel them being inserted.

You may notice sensations such as warmth, tingling, heaviness, or movement around a point. Many patients become so relaxed that they fall asleep during treatment.

## What is the Balance Method?

The Balance Method is an acupuncture system developed by Dr. Richard Tan that is often used for pain relief and internal health concerns.

One of its unique features is that needles are often placed away from the area of pain. This allows treatment to be comfortable, especially if the painful area is swollen, bruised or tender,  while often producing noticeable changes in pain, mobility, and function.

## What is Chinese herbal medicine?

Chinese herbal medicine uses combinations of herbs that are selected based on your individual presentation, symptoms, constitution, and health goals.

Herbs may be used alone or alongside acupuncture and are commonly recommended for women's health, fertility, digestive concerns, sleep, stress, and overall wellness.

At Tapestry Holistic Healing, only professional-grade herbal products from reputable manufacturers are recommended. These companies use rigorous quality control standards and test their products for identity, potency, purity, and safety. Products are screened for contaminants such as heavy metals, pesticides, microbes, and other impurities to help ensure quality and effectiveness.

Not all herbal products are created equal. Choosing herbs from trusted professional suppliers helps ensure that you receive products that meet high standards for safety and consistency.

## What makes Functional Medicine different?

Functional Medicine is a personalized approach to health that focuses on identifying and addressing potential root causes rather than simply managing symptoms.

Functional Medicine is about connecting the threads. Symptoms rarely occur in isolation. By exploring the relationships between hormones, digestion, nutrition, sleep, stress, metabolism, and lifestyle, we can gain a clearer picture of what may be contributing to imbalance and where opportunities for healing exist.

  

We look at how factors such as nutrition, digestion, hormones, blood sugar regulation, sleep, stress, lifestyle, environmental exposures, and nutrient status may be contributing to how you feel.

Functional Medicine also takes a different approach to laboratory testing. While conventional medicine often focuses on whether a result falls within a broad "normal" range, Functional Medicine uses narrower optimal ranges to help identify patterns and opportunities for improvement before they become more significant health concerns.

The goal is not simply the absence of disease. The goal is helping you feel and function at your best through personalized recommendations that support long-term health, vitality, and wellbeing.

## Do you offer functional lab testing?

Yes. Depending on your health goals, recommendations may include hormone testing, fertility testing, gut health testing, food sensitivity testing, nutritional assessments, and functional blood chemistry review.

Testing is only recommended when it is likely to provide information that will help guide your care.

## Do I need testing to work with you?

No.

Many patients see excellent results through acupuncture, herbal medicine, nutrition, and lifestyle changes alone. Testing is simply one tool that can provide additional information when needed.

## Do you work with fertility patients?

Yes.

Fertility support has been a major focus of my work for more than 20 years. Care may include acupuncture, functional testing recommendations, nutrition, supplements, cycle tracking, IVF support, and preconception health optimization.

## Can acupuncture help during IVF?

Many fertility patients choose to include acupuncture before and during IVF treatment.

Acupuncture is commonly used to support relaxation, stress management, nervous system regulation, and overall reproductive health throughout the fertility journey.

## Do you work with women in perimenopause and menopause?

Absolutely.

Many women seek support for symptoms such as fatigue, sleep disturbances, weight gain, hot flashes, brain fog, anxiety, and hormone-related changes.

Treatment plans may include acupuncture, herbal medicine, functional medicine, nutrition, and lifestyle recommendations.

## What is Energy Medicine?

Energy Medicine is a gentle healing modality that works with the body's energetic systems to support balance, relaxation, and wellbeing.

Some people seek Energy Medicine for stress reduction and emotional healing. Others are interested in personal growth, spiritual development, or navigating major life transitions.

Energy Medicine can be received on its own or combined with an acupuncture treatment.

## Do I need to be spiritual to benefit from Energy Medicine?

No.

Some clients approach Energy Medicine from a spiritual perspective, while others simply enjoy the relaxation and sense of wellbeing they experience during a session.

## How many treatments will I need?

That depends on your goals, health history, and how long the issue has been present.

Acute concerns often require fewer but initially more frequent treatments than chronic or long-standing conditions. During your first visit, we will discuss a treatment plan that makes sense for your situation.

## Do you accept insurance?

Tapestry Holistic Healing is currently in network with Regence.

If you do not have Regence and your insurance plan offers out-of-network acupuncture benefits, a superbill can be provided for possible reimbursement. HSA and FSA cards are accepted for eligible services.

## Where are you located?

Tapestry Holistic Healing is located in Lake Oswego, Oregon and serves patients from Lake Oswego, West Linn, Tualatin, Tigard, Portland, and surrounding communities.

## How do I get started?

Schedule a new patient appointment through our online booking system.

We will review your health history, discuss your goals, and create a personalized plan to support your health and wellbeing.

# Meet Christy Mahoney

My journey into holistic medicine began long before I became a practitioner. As a teenager and young adult, I struggled with painful periods, chronic fatigue, digestive issues, and later insomnia. Like many people searching for answers, I found myself moving from appointment to appointment, often feeling unheard and frustrated that my symptoms were being managed rather than understood.

That experience sparked a lifelong fascination with health, healing, and the remarkable ability of the body to restore balance when given the right support.

I earned my Bachelor's degree in Biology from Villanova University before completing my Master's degree in Traditional Chinese Medicine at the American College of Traditional Chinese Medicine in San Francisco in 2002. Over the past 24 years, I have continued to expand my training through functional medicine, nutrition, herbal medicine, fertility, and energy medicine. I am a Functional Diagnostic Nutrition Practitioner (FDN-P) and was an ABORM Fellow for nearly two decades.

Throughout my career, I have had the privilege of helping people navigate fertility challenges, women's health concerns, digestive disorders, chronic pain, stress-related conditions, sleep issues, and complex health concerns that often do not fit neatly into a conventional medical diagnosis.

What I love most about this work is the opportunity to look beyond symptoms and ask deeper questions.

Why is the body struggling?

What underlying patterns are contributing to imbalance?

What changes can create meaningful, lasting improvement?

My approach combines the wisdom of Chinese medicine with the detective work of functional medicine. Rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all solution, I work to uncover potential root causes and create personalized recommendations that may include acupuncture, herbal medicine, functional testing, nutrition, supplementation, lifestyle support, and energy medicine.

I believe healing is about much more than symptom relief. It is about understanding your body, reconnecting with yourself, and developing the tools to support your health for years to come.

The name Tapestry Holistic Healing reflects this philosophy. A tapestry is created by weaving many individual threads together to form something stronger, richer, and more beautiful than any single thread alone. In much the same way, our health is influenced by physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual factors that are deeply interconnected. When we address these pieces together, profound healing becomes possible.

I am especially passionate about helping women navigate fertility, perimenopause, and the many transitions that occur throughout life. I also connect deeply with those who view healing as part of a larger journey of personal growth and self-discovery.

Outside of the clinic, you'll usually find me hiking, kayaking, practicing yoga, spending time with my husband, teenage son, and our Alaskan Malamute, Loa, or enjoying the outdoors that make the Pacific Northwest such a special place to call home. I also have a longstanding interest in meditation, intuitive development, writing, and spiritual exploration. And despite living on the West Coast for many years, I remain a loyal Philadelphia Eagles fan.

After stepping away from private practice to homeschool my son during the pandemic, I felt called to return to in-person patient care and create the kind of healing space I had always envisioned—a calm, grounded sanctuary where people can slow down, feel supported, and receive truly personalized care.

It would be my honor to be part of your healing journey.
