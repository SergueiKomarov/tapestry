Acupuncture & Herbal Medicine

Traditional Chinese Medicine practices to balance your body's energy flow, address root causes, and support whole-body healing through centuries of wisdom.

Acupuncture is one of the oldest and most widely practiced forms of medicine in the world, with roots stretching back more than 2,500 years in Chinese medicine tradition.

At Tapestry Holistic Healing, acupuncture is offered as part of a comprehensive, integrative approach to your health — not as a standalone treatment, but as one thread within the larger tapestry of your care. Christy Mahoney has been practicing acupuncture for nearly 24 years and brings deep clinical experience, advanced training, and a genuine commitment to helping each patient achieve lasting, meaningful results.

Acupuncture

Fine, sterile needles are placed at specific points on the body to regulate the flow of energy (Qi), reduce inflammation, support nervous system balance, and stimulate the body's natural healing responses.

The Balance Method

A specialized acupuncture system developed by Dr. Richard Tan, often used for pain relief and internal health concerns. Needles are placed away from the area of pain, allowing comfortable treatment while often producing immediate changes in pain and mobility.

Chinese Herbal Medicine

Custom herbal formulas selected based on your individual constitution, symptoms, and health goals. Only professional-grade products from manufacturers with rigorous quality control standards are recommended.
