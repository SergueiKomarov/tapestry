Energy Medicine

A gentle yet powerful healing modality that works with the body's subtle energetic systems to support balance, emotional healing, stress relief, and profound transformation.

Book an Appointment

At Tapestry Holistic Healing, I believe that true healing extends beyond the physical body. Energy Medicine is one of the most profound ways to support that deeper healing.

Many people come to energy medicine after realizing that they have addressed the physical aspects of their health yet still feel that something is missing. Sometimes the missing piece is not another supplement, another protocol, or another treatment plan. Sometimes it is a deeper connection to yourself, to your inner wisdom, or to something greater than yourself.

Energy Medicine creates space for that connection — and for remembering who you really are.

What Is Energy Medicine?

Energy Medicine works with the body's subtle energetic systems to encourage balance, flow, and healing. It is informed by more than two decades of training, including Reiki Master certification (2002), study with renowned healer Rosalyn Bruyere, and ongoing development through years of integrated clinical practice.

What to Expect

Many clients describe their sessions as deeply relaxing, restorative, and transformative. Some experience a profound sense of peace, clarity, emotional release, or renewed connection to themselves. Others simply leave feeling lighter, calmer, and more grounded. Each session is unique and unfolds according to the needs of the individual.

Combined with Acupuncture

Because acupuncture and energy healing work beautifully together, energetic techniques can often be integrated into an acupuncture session to support deeper relaxation, emotional processing, nervous system regulation, and overall healing. Standalone Energy Medicine sessions are also available.

Energy Medicine May Support

  • Stress and overwhelm
  • Emotional healing and processing
  • Life transitions and major changes
  • Burnout and depletion
  • Chronic illness support
  • Feeling disconnected from oneself
  • Spiritual growth and self-discovery
  • Energetic sensitivity and boundaries
  • Deep relaxation and nervous system regulation
  • Personal transformation and inner work

The Missing Thread

You do not need to be spiritually oriented to benefit from Energy Medicine. Some clients approach it from a spiritual perspective; others simply enjoy the relaxation and sense of wellbeing they experience. All are welcome.
