Fertility and Preconception Support in Lake Oswego

Individualized support for your fertility journey, whether you are preparing for pregnancy or pursuing assisted reproduction.

Book an Appointment

Fertility care is personal. At Tapestry Holistic Healing in Lake Oswego, Christy Mahoney, LAc, MSTCM, FDN‑P, offers support tailored to your health history and goals.

Care may include acupuncture, nutrition and lifestyle guidance, cycle tracking, and discussion of functional testing or supplements. The approach depends on your individual circumstances.

Preconception and IVF Support

Whether you are trying to conceive or preparing for IVF, Christy can discuss how acupuncture and other individualized care may fit into your plans. Acupuncture may be used for relaxation and stress management during the process.

Fertility support is complementary and does not replace evaluation or treatment by your medical provider or fertility specialist. Discuss your care, herbs, and supplements with your medical team, especially during pregnancy or fertility treatment. No outcome can be guaranteed.

Getting Started

Bring your questions and health history to your appointment so you can discuss your goals and options. You can also explore our women's health and functional medicine pages.
