Functional Medicine

A personalized, root-cause approach that connects the threads — exploring how hormones, digestion, nutrition, sleep, stress, and lifestyle all contribute to how you feel and function.

Book an Appointment

Functional Medicine is about connecting the threads. Symptoms rarely occur in isolation — and addressing them in isolation rarely leads to lasting change.

Rather than focusing solely on what your symptoms are called, Functional Medicine asks: why are they happening? What underlying patterns are contributing to imbalance? And what changes can create meaningful, lasting improvement?

This approach uses the detective work of investigating root causes combined with the wisdom of understanding how all the systems of the body interrelate — from gut microbiome to hormone signaling, from sleep quality to metabolic function.

Root-Cause Investigation

Rather than managing symptoms with a one-size-fits-all approach, we explore the interconnected factors that may be driving your health concerns — including nutrition, digestion, hormones, blood sugar, sleep, stress, and environmental influences.

Functional Lab Testing

Functional Medicine uses optimal laboratory ranges (not just "normal") to identify patterns and opportunities for improvement before they become more significant health concerns. Testing may include hormone panels, gut health assessments, food sensitivity testing, and comprehensive blood chemistry.

Personalized Recommendations

No two people are the same. Recommendations are tailored to your unique constitution, health history, and goals — and may include targeted nutrition, supplementation, lifestyle strategies, and integration with acupuncture and herbal medicine.

Areas of Focus

  • Hormone imbalances and thyroid health
  • Blood sugar regulation and metabolic health
  • Digestive health and gut microbiome
  • Adrenal health, fatigue and burnout
  • Sleep optimization
  • Inflammatory conditions
  • Nutrient deficiencies and supplementation
  • Preconception and fertility optimization
  • Perimenopause and menopause support
  • Chronic conditions that haven't responded to conventional care

Discover the Root Cause

The goal of Functional Medicine is not simply the absence of disease — it is helping you feel and function at your best through personalized care that supports long-term health, vitality, and wellbeing.
