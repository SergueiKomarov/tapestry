Women's Health

Specialized, compassionate support for fertility, IVF preparation, perimenopause, menopause, and all stages of a woman's health journey through integrative care.

Book an Appointment

Women's health has been at the heart of Christy Mahoney's practice for more than 20 years — from fertility and preconception care to the transitions of perimenopause and beyond.

Many women come seeking care after feeling like a number in a system that wasn't designed to look at them as a whole person. The integrative approach at Tapestry Holistic Healing is different: each patient is heard, each situation is unique, and care is crafted to support not just symptoms, but the whole woman — physically, hormonally, and emotionally.

Fertility & Preconception

Comprehensive fertility support including acupuncture, functional testing, nutrition, supplements, cycle optimization, and preconception health preparation. Care is tailored to your individual fertility journey — whether you are trying naturally or preparing for IVF.

IVF Support

Acupuncture is commonly integrated into IVF protocols to support relaxation, stress management, nervous system regulation, and overall reproductive health throughout the process — before retrieval, before transfer, and during the two-week wait.

Perimenopause & Menopause

Integrative support for the hormonal transitions of midlife, including fatigue, sleep disturbances, weight changes, hot flashes, brain fog, anxiety, and mood shifts — using acupuncture, herbal medicine, functional medicine, and nutrition.

Hormonal Health

Support for PMS, painful or irregular periods, endometriosis, PCOS, thyroid concerns, and broader hormonal imbalances through root-cause investigation and individualized treatment planning.

Areas of Focus

  • Fertility optimization and preconception care
  • IVF and assisted reproduction support
  • Recurrent pregnancy loss
  • PMS, painful periods, and irregular cycles
  • Endometriosis and PCOS
  • Perimenopause symptoms
  • Menopause transition and hormone balancing
  • Hot flashes, sleep disturbances, brain fog
  • Thyroid and adrenal health
  • Stress and anxiety related to women's health
  • Postpartum support

You Deserve to Feel Like Yourself

Whether you are navigating fertility, hormonal shifts, or the transitions of midlife, you deserve care that honors the complexity and wisdom of your body. Christy would be honored to be part of your journey.
